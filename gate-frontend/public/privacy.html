Privacy & data

GATE Studio is a family study application. Papers, attempts, results and connection settings are stored in your browser so saved tests can work offline.

Profiles

Local profiles separate workspaces on this browser. They are not password-protected accounts. Google profiles use the name, email and account identifier returned by Google after you choose to sign in.

Google Drive

When enabled by the site owner and authorized by you, the app can read files you select with Google Picker and create or update its own progress-backup files in your Google Drive. The app requests per-file Drive access, not full-drive access. Google access tokens stay in this browser session's memory and are not stored in progress backups or published to GitHub.

Shared files and local AI

The private family connection file contains the local AI access key. It is saved in this browser and sent only to the configured AI companion. Questions you ask the local tutor are sent to the host computer running the model. The public website configuration contains no family access key.

Control and deletion

You can disconnect the Drive session, revoke app access in your Google account, delete app backups in your Drive, or clear this site's browser data. Clearing browser data removes local attempts and saved settings; export or back up needed work first. Disconnecting does not delete your local or Drive files.

Contact the person who shared this site with you for questions about the family folder or host computer.
